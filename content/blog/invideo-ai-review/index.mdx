<h2>What is InVideo AI?</h2>
<p>InVideo AI is an AI-powered video creation platform that turns text instructions into professional-looking videos. Unlike traditional video editors, InVideo AI uses advanced AI to automate the entire video creation process — from scriptwriting to scene selection, voiceover generation, and final editing.</p>
<p>After testing many AI video tools over the past year, I was particularly impressed by InVideo AI’s balance between automation and creative control. It’s designed for content creators, marketers, and businesses that need to produce video content quickly without sacrificing quality.</p>
</section>

<section data-animate="">
<h2>Key features we tested</h2>
<h3>1. AI script generator</h3>
<p>The AI scriptwriter is surprisingly capable. I tested it with prompts ranging from “explain blockchain technology” to “create a recipe video for a chocolate cake.” The generated scripts were coherent, well structured, and required minimal edits.</p>
<div class="feature-example">
<picture>
<img alt="InVideo AI script generation interface" decoding="async" height="2027" loading="lazy" src="/images/capture-features.png" width="3702"/>
</picture>
<p class="caption">AI Script Generator interface — simple yet effective</p>
</div>
<h3>2. Text-to-video conversion</h3>
<p>This is where InVideo AI really shines. The platform automatically breaks your script into scenes, selects relevant stock footage, adds transitions, and syncs everything with voiceovers. Scene selection is context-aware and smart.</p>
<h3>3. Template library</h3>
<p>With 5,000+ templates optimized for different platforms (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, etc.), you’ll find strong starting points for almost any type of video. The templates are professionally designed and save considerable time.</p>
<h3>4. AI voiceover</h3>
<p>The speech engine offers 50+ voices in multiple languages. While some sound slightly robotic, the premium voices are quite natural. You can adjust speaking rate and add emotional tones.</p>
<h3>5. Brand kit</h3>
<p>For businesses, the brand kit feature helps keep videos consistent by saving logos, colors, and fonts. It’s especially helpful for creating video series.</p>
</section>
